# Market / Competitive Positioning (Not a Sales Forecast)

## Current established competitors

| Vendor | Publicly documented strengths (2026-10 primary references) | Our *hypothesis* for differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| Photoroom | Backgrounds, batch, fidelity QA, enterprise workflows and ecommerce assets | Developer-transparent BYOK, local portable projects, exact artwork compositing workflow |
| Pebblely | Product upload and text-aware preservation, themes, bulk generation and downloads | Raw original-layer preservation + explicit QA veto / full attempt cost lineage |
| Flair.ai | Drag/drop composition, templates, brand assets, product photos/videos | Lightweight installable local-first PWA + simple seven-style workflow |
| Shopify AI editor | Integrated background/scene editing within shop product media | Platform-agnostic export and open source-style project portability |

**Warning:** These vendors already support high-quality photo editing and often strive for accuracy. Brand Lock alone is not proven as a unique competitive moat; verify with head-to-head empirical test. Avoid blanket claim "competitors cannot preserve product".

## Positioning statement

"AI Product Image Studio BYOK helps independent sellers turn real source photos into selected brand-reviewed commerce visuals with a documented original-image preservation path, user-controlled provider spend and portable local projects."

## Distribution / monetization hypotheses

- Open public demo using synthetic assets and mock provider; self-host/hosted BYOK for more technical audience.
- Later optional managed convenience tier, team Brand Kits, ecommerce output presets and listing-copy tooling.
- Before pricing or growth work, compare measured cost-per-approved asset, seller time saved, trust, repeat workflow and privacy objections against alternatives.

## Go/no-go criteria

Proceed beyond pilot if multi-category brand-fidelity and cost benchmark meets explicit seller acceptance, secure BYOK gate is feasible to operate, and enough target sellers choose the local-first control benefits. Hold if image source fidelity is poor, masking effort dominates, API charges unpredictable or hosted-key custody untrusted.
