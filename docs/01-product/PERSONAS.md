# Personas and Jobs To Be Done

## P-01 Solo seller (SG/MY)

- Channel: Shopee / Lazada / Shopify / social listing, limited photo budget, mostly mobile.
- Goal: upload a product picture, get accurate backgrounds and shop-ready images, avoid fake claims and hidden spend.
- Constraints: product may have dense Chinese/English printed labels, inconsistent lighting, only a front view.
- Success: one approved Studio picture within an acceptable user-defined cost and effort; **do not** measure generation success alone.

## P-02 Freelancer / product photographer

- Goal: reuse prompt presets for a brand, quickly compare seven styles and export selected approved images.
- Needs: prompt versioning, variants, mask correction, QA annotations and export metadata.
- Avoid: uncontrolled full regeneration of labels, loss of source files, manual bookkeeping of each retry.

## P-03 Technical merchant / developer

- Goal: local ownership of project files, own OpenAI spending and self-hosted proxy option.
- Needs: transparent API contracts, audit trails, ZIP portability, security posture and upgrade path to product listing automation.
- Avoid: proprietary non-exportable image history or a hidden vendor image API markup.

## Negative personas / non-target

- Brands that need fully verified regulatory artwork without professional approval; cannot use AI as replacement for legal sign-off.
- Large enterprise catalog with SSO, approval chain, DAM and a thousand concurrent jobs: later expansion.
- Users seeking fully autonomous invented reviews/health claims/testimonials: explicitly out of scope.

## Segmentation hypotheses to validate

1. Product-label fidelity will matter more for packaged goods than plain unbranded furniture.
2. Cost control attracts developers and repeat users; ordinary merchants may prefer fixed-fee SaaS.
3. Mobile-first workflow is critical, but detailed mask/QA editing may work better on desktop.

All three are hypotheses; run a pilot rather than claiming proven market demand.
